Honest caveats

  • Onset RMSE 933 ms against the constant-tempo null's 4743 ms (-80%). RMSE is dominated by accumulated drift, not by local error: a small constant tempo bias integrates over the window.
  • Velocity RMSE 11.12 against the null's 40.50 (-73%).
  • This is a synthetic record: its performance was rendered from a known MPM, so the model is being asked to invert a process it was trained on. Real-data behaviour is the Vienna probe, not this page.
  • The pedal head's state MAE is contaminated on synthetic data — the target leaks into input feature 14. It is a known v1.0 design correction, so read the pedal number as diagnostic only.
  • No movementMap in the emitted document. Pedal is predicted per note as a state; turning states back into movement instructions is the v1.1 reconstruction pass (SYSTEM.md §4). Ornaments and imprecision are likewise out of the v1.0 vocabulary. Where a sustain-CC number is reported it therefore scores a document with no pedal in it — it is the no-pedal null, not a pedal prediction.
  • Instruction-space agreement is partial here — tempo boundary F1 0.71, dynamics boundary F1 0.50, rubato span F1 0.00 (±1 beat tolerance). That is the expected shape of the problem, not a contradiction of the render numbers: several different instruction sets render nearly the same performance, and the MDL ratio says this one spends about as many tokens as the truth did (0.98 of the ground truth's subset cost).
  • The articulationMap's values are the heads' raw regression outputs — unrounded floats, where the canonical form is 2 decimals for relativeDuration and an integer for absoluteVelocityChange (CANONICAL A2/A3). They are written as predicted because they are what was rendered and scored; a canonicalisation pass belongs in the export path.
  • Single seed, single checkpoint (runs/v41-asyn-h100/ckpt.pt, epoch 95, 4,282,791 parameters, 16 features, heads on), trained purely on synthetic renderings. One record is an illustration, never evidence — the evidence is the 1000-piece validation and the 220-window Vienna probe in ml/LOG.md.
